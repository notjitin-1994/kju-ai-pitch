# Taste Skill

## Overview
A skill that gives your AI agent good taste and prevents it from generating boring, generic UI output. Focuses on developing design sensibility in AI systems.

## Purpose
Stops AI from generating bland, forgettable interfaces by encoding what makes design "good." Helps AI understand the subtle qualities that elevate a design from adequate to excellent.

## Core Philosophy

Good taste is not about personal preference—it's a trained instinct developed by:
1. Surrounding yourself with great work
2. Thinking deeply about why something feels good
3. Practicing design decisions relentlessly

## What It Teaches AI

The skill encodes recognition of:
- Visual hierarchy and contrast
- Intentional use of whitespace
- Thoughtful typography choices
- Cohesive color usage
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Consistency in details
- Psychological aspects of good design

## Key Principles

- Design is about details that compound
- Taste differentiates in a world where everything is "good enough"
- Every element should have intentional purpose
- Restraint is a design virtue
- Motion should feel natural and purposeful
- Colors should work as a system

## When to Use
- When designing beautiful interfaces
- When refining existing designs
- When you want to prevent generic output
- When teaching AI systems about good taste
